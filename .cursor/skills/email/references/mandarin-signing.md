# Mandarin, customer signing follow up

Use as a reference when drafting Mandarin emails to customers or partners, especially when the recipient is senior or an elder. Keep the voice warm and human, not corporate. Use 您 (polite second person) and light honorifics like 贵司, but avoid stiff business phrasing such as 拨冗, 谨附, 此致敬礼 unless the user asks for formal tone.

Notes on tone:
- Open with a simple thank you tied to what just happened (a call, a meeting), not a generic greeting.
- State the reason for writing in the next line.
- Keep instructions and examples visually clean. A short labeled block works better than prose for form fields.
- Close with appreciation for the recipient's patience or partnership, then sign off with just the first name.
- No dashes. Use commas, colons, or new sentences.

Example:

主题：感谢今天的通话

您好，

谢谢您今天抽空和我们通话。
为了让签署流程更顺一些，附上一个填写示例给您参考：

By: 陳小明
Name: 陳小明
Title: CEO
Date: 5/27/2026

我也把需要签署的部分截了图附在邮件里，方便您对照。如果贵司习惯加盖公司章，也欢迎一并盖上。
我们距离完成只剩最后一步了，真的很感谢您和团队这段时间的耐心。

Joseph
