# Example: expanding a training invite to a wider team

Pattern: warm greeting with in person callback, brief apology for a missed meeting with a real reason, the ask early (extend the invite broadly), shared context that motivates the ask, why the session is worth it and how it generalizes, sequencing note (101 before 201), restated ask, gracious close.
